## Full brief

The owner's complete instructions follow verbatim in sections 1–12 below.

1. **Inspect and improve the existing app.** Map what works, what is incomplete and what will change, then implement. Reuse the framework, styles, components and services; no rewrite. Preserve historical records, identifiers, attachments, categories, splits and relationships; additive schema changes only, with backup/recovery. Old receipts without items stay valid. Configurable settings for unspecified preferences (e.g. payday).
2. **Fast capture.** Add always reachable; Scan and Manual paths; amount, merchant, date, account, currency easy; More details for the rest. Date/account/currency/More categories reachable on narrow phones (scroll or wrap, never clip). Explicit close/back; drafts survive dismissal and interruption. Keyboard and Save never obscure needed fields; familiar decimal entry. Save confirmed totals immediately; item corrections can continue in a review queue. Success only after a durable local save, distinct from sync; no duplicate submits; Undo and retry. Match a scan to an existing manual transaction (and imports).
3. **Receipt items.** Store receipt relation, original item text, editable normalised description, category/subcategory, line amount, review status; quantity, unit, pack size, unit price, discounts, tax when present. Never invent. Mixed receipts (groceries, household, personal care). Grocery subcategories: produce, dairy, meat/fish, pantry, snacks, beverages (editable, consistent with existing categories). Phone item-review list against the image; corrections are never silently overwritten; correction scope defaults to the item/transaction, rules for future purchases are explicit, historical bulk changes are previewed. Cautious product grouping; unit-price comparisons only on comparable units. Receipt total distinct from extraction completeness; discounts/tax without double counting; never count both total and items; visible unallocated amount with a correction path for shortfall or excess. Item reports and AI state coverage plus an unknown amount; reprocessing only when an image exists, with review. Drilldowns: top merchants, grocery subcategories, recurring products, changes across comparable salary cycles; every total leads to its receipts and items.
4. **Salary-to-salary cycles** as the default in Home, Activity, Budgets, Savings, reports and AI, with explicit start/end dates and days left. Configurable payday with early/late adjustment; short months and timezone handled; not every income is salary. Purchase date decides the cycle; one boundary rule; historical boundaries preserved unless a change is previewed. Compare completed with completed, active with the same elapsed portion; show the basis. Calendar month and custom range stay as secondary views.
5. **Shared expenses.** Full amount, payer, my share, others' shares, owed, settlements; no accounts needed for other people. Equal, percentage, fixed and item-level splits that reconcile exactly (odd cents, discounts), with visible rounding. Personal share is the default basis for spending reports and budgets; a full-amount view for cash movement; available money reflects what actually left my accounts. Settlements and reimbursements attach to the obligation (not purchases, not salary). Relationships survive edits, refunds and sync; reversible.
6. **AI assistant with evidence.** Arithmetic, conversion, splits, totals, comparisons and forecasts come from the calculation layer; AI queries and explains. Example questions: top supermarket this cycle; where grocery money went; what explains the change vs the previous comparable period; buying more vs paying more per comparable unit; what I owe and am owed; records or recurring charges needing review; effect of spending and commitments on budgets and goals. Answers state period, currency, share/full basis, coverage and supporting receipts/items; separate facts from uncertain extraction. Bookkeeping actions (categories, duplicates, recurring rules, find records, prepare corrections) are previewed before applying, traceable and undoable; never execute real payments. Short insight summary plus follow-up chat; loading/error/retry/insufficient-data states; insights invalidated or labelled when sources change; no AI call per render.
7. **Outbound data boundary.** Aggregates for aggregate questions, detail only when needed, pseudonymous account/participant IDs. Strip names, account/card numbers, payment/loyalty IDs, addresses, precise branch/location, image metadata, free-text notes by default. Raw images handled separately under an explicit, accurately described setting with redaction where needed; manual entry and deferred processing always available. A way to inspect what is sent; describe it as minimised/pseudonymised. Keys never in prompts, bundles or logs. Keep the configured provider (DeepSeek).
8. **Offline.** Reopen offline; manual entry, photo capture/import, drafts, editing, search, splits and local reports offline. Persist records and files before confirming. Cloud OCR/AI work is queued and labelled "waiting for connection"; a saved photo awaiting extraction is distinct from a confirmed expense; manual total while extraction is pending; past insights shown with timestamp. Durable storage, cached app shell, honest storage-failure handling, backup/export/restore (browser storage is not a backup). Persistent outbox with stable operation IDs; retry on reconnect and foreground plus manual retry; retries never create duplicates; extraction results never overwrite newer manual corrections; cancellation/deletion is never uploaded later; explicit conflict resolution for amounts, shares, dates and deletes; drafts and queue survive restarts and updates. Statuses: Saved on this device, Waiting for connection, Synced, Needs attention; separate states for record, image and analysis.
9. **Travel currencies and trips.** Original amount and currency plus EUR amount, rate, date/source, confirmed vs estimated; prefer the actual charged EUR when known. Offline: manual/cached rate or pending conversion; never assume 1:1; incomplete conversion visible in totals; no silent historical revaluation. Same basis for items, splits, settlements, budgets and AI; rounding reconciled. Trip tagging by transaction date and scope; recurring bills and late scans not auto-tagged; trip views keep personal share and exclusions explicit.
10. **Layouts.** Keep the visual identity. Phone: flexible layouts, real scrolling, reachable filters, safe areas, keyboard-aware forms, 44–48 px targets, readable secondary text, enlarged text, labels/focus/selected/error states. Home: current pay cycle, defined spending, upcoming commitments, review queue, one useful insight, recent activity; savings and trips without crowding capture; any allowance/forecast shows its basis. Fix real navigation inconsistencies; Settings has a proper destination; filters and scroll survive back; edits default to the current record. Tablet: list/detail and receipt/review panes. PC: transaction table + detail pane with keyboard support. Same financial rules on every device.
11. **Motion.** Shared tokens. Press/selection 100–150 ms; expand/filter/inline 160–220 ms preserving position and focus; sheets/navigation 220–300 ms, restrained easing; capture→review with real extraction state, no fake progress; save acknowledged immediately with Undo, never waiting on animation or AI; list insert/edit/delete subtle without jumping; charts animate on selection change only, values shown immediately; honest AI/sync states without endless pulsing. Ease-out entrances, opacity/transform, interruptible, never blocks input or hides failures; no stale amounts under a new period; no duplicate saves on repeated taps or orientation change. Reduced motion: remove travel/scale/stagger/nonessential chart motion, keep feedback; optional app setting. No confetti, parallax, long staggers, heavy blur, bouncing.
12. **Verify with synthetic records** (never modify real history): offline capture + restart + reconnect → one expense; manual then scan → attach; verified total with unresolved items → correct totals, visible coverage; mixed supermarket receipt → correct split, drilldown, no double count; odd-cent equal and item splits → reconcile, personal vs cash differs correctly; payday boundary incl. short month and early/late salary → right cycle after late sync; foreign currency offline → original kept, missing EUR visible; one item recategorised → no unrelated history changes, bulk rule needs scope and is reversible; retry after interruption + edit on another device → no duplicate, lost correction or resurrected delete; "what drove grocery spending" → right period, share basis, items, honest coverage; inspect outgoing AI data → identifiers and unauthorised images excluded; phone/tablet/PC with long names, large text, keyboard, empty/error/loading, reduced motion → actions reachable; migrated records, accounts, budgets, goals, investments, recurring bills, trips, attachments keep their relationships.

Report: what was already present, what changed, what was tested, remaining limitations, screen previews. No placeholder buttons, fabricated AI results, simulated sync, or plan-only deliverables.
