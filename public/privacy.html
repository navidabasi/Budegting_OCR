Privacy Policy

Where My Money Went?

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how "Where My Money Went?" ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials.
  • Receipt Data: Information you input or scan, including merchant names, dates, amounts, and categories.
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics about how you use the App to improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Sync your data across your devices
  • Process receipt images using OCR technology
  • Improve and optimize the App
  • Communicate with you about your account

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices:

  • Data is stored locally on your device for offline access
  • Cloud data is stored on Supabase with encryption at rest
  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • We implement row-level security to ensure you can only access your own data

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: For authentication and cloud data storage
  • Google Sign-In: For optional social authentication
  • OCR.space: For receipt text recognition (images are processed and not stored)

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only:

  • With your consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and safety

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Export your data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your data at any time through the App settings. Upon account deletion, your data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the App or via email.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@wheremymoneywent.app
